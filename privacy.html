IvuRadar Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

IvuRadar ("the app") is a Windows desktop app that helps you keep track of job interviews in your email and calendar. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • The app runs on your computer and keeps what it reads on your computer.
  • The developer has no server that receives your emails, calendar or account details.
  • Your data is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for advertising.

Data the app accesses from Google

When you connect a Gmail account, you sign in with Google and grant the app these permissions:

  • Gmail, read-only (gmail.readonly): the app reads your emails to find interview requests, invitations, offers, rejections and emailed verification codes for booking pages you open in the app.
  • Gmail, send (gmail.send): the app sends the replies, follow-ups and messages you choose to send from it.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.readonly, calendar.events): the app shows your meetings, adds interviews to your calendar, and accepts or declines invitations when you ask it to.

The app also uses your Google account's email address to label the profile it belongs to.

How the data is used and stored

  • Emails and calendar events the app reads are used only to provide its features: finding interviews, reminders, drafts, reports and your to-do list.
  • They are stored in the app's data folder on your computer. Google sign-in tokens are encrypted with your Windows account's protection before they are saved.
  • Emails are sent only when you choose to send them, or when you turn on automatic replies or follow-ups in the app's settings.
  • No person reads your data, other than you.

When data leaves your computer

  • Google: to read and send email and to read and update your calendar, as described above.
  • Anthropic (optional): only if you enter your own Anthropic API key in the app's settings, the text of interview emails is sent to Anthropic's API to classify them and to draft replies. Without a key, this is done by an AI model that runs on your own computer (Ollama), and nothing is sent.
  • Company information (optional): when you ask for information about a company, the app looks up the company's public website and Wikipedia using the company's name and web address. Your emails are not sent.
  • Web pages you open: booking and application pages you open in the app's built-in browser receive what you type or ask the app to fill in.

Google API Services User Data Policy

IvuRadar's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is not used to develop, improve or train general AI models, is not transferred to anyone except as described above to provide the app's features, and is not used for advertising.

Your choices

  • Remove a profile in the app to delete its stored emails, calendar data and Google sign-in from your computer. The app's activity log (the Logs page) keeps its record of what was done, such as email subjects, until you delete the app's data folder.
  • Uninstall the app and delete its data folder to remove everything it stored.
  • Revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

The app is meant for adults looking for work and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
