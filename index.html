IvuRadar

A Windows desktop app for job seekers: it finds interview requests in your Gmail, keeps your interviews on your calendar, and helps you answer on time.

What it does

  • Finds interview requests, invitations, offers and rejections among your emails.
  • Shows today's interviews, the invitations waiting for your answer, and the requests waiting for your reply.
  • Adds interviews to your Google Calendar and accepts or declines invitations when you ask it to.
  • Drafts replies (your free times, follow-ups) that you review and send from the app.
  • Reports how your job search is going: requests received, replies, interviews scheduled.

How it uses your Google account

IvuRadar runs on your own computer. When you connect a Gmail account, it asks Google for permission to:

  • Read your email (Gmail, read-only), to find interview-related emails.
  • Send email (Gmail, send), for replies and follow-ups you choose to send from the app.
  • See and add calendar events (Google Calendar), to show your interviews and add or answer the ones you choose.

What the app reads is kept on your computer. It is never sent to the developer, sold, or used for advertising. Details are in the privacy policy.
